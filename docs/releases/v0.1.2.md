- Much less network traffic: show details no longer download the full cast history (which runs to megabytes for long-running shows), only two shows' details load at a time, and only for the shows near the cursor.
- Channel listings come first: background details pause while a channel loads, and the first 20 shows appear as soon as they arrive.
- Each connection is separate (HTTP/1.1), so one slow download can't hold up the rest, and a stalled connection is dropped before retrying.
- The tuning screen says when TMDB is slow to answer, and the log records slow requests.
- API keys are left out of the log and error messages.
